Captain’s Log is available under two licensing paths.

(`AGPL-3.0-only`). The complete terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The AGPL path permits commercial use. It also imposes its copyleft and source-availability
conditions, including the network-interaction requirements in section 13. Nothing in this summary
changes the licence text.

## Commercial licences

Solomonoff Consultancy Ltd may separately negotiate a commercial licence for organisations that
need terms different from AGPL-3.0-only. No commercial rights are granted unless both parties enter
into a separate written agreement.

Commercial licences can cover only rights controlled by Solomonoff Consultancy Ltd. Third-party
dependencies, trademarks, compatible-device firmware and other third-party material remain subject
to their own terms. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).

Accepted contributions remain available in the AGPL-licensed project. To preserve the commercial
licensing path, code and documentation contributions require the applicable Harmony Option Five

- [Individual CLA](CLA/individual.md)

Contributors retain copyright. The CLA grants Solomonoff Consultancy Ltd the additional rights
needed to distribute contributions under AGPL and separately negotiated commercial terms.
